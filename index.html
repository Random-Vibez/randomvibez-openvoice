OpenVoice Board

Tap a card to hear a phrase. The curated vocabulary is the same for every visitor and cannot be edited or uploaded.

A simple way to express more

OpenVoice Board is a straightforward communication aid for people who may need another way to express needs, feelings, choices, comfort, or conversation. It works without an account and keeps the available vocabulary fixed, so the public tool is predictable and cannot be changed by strangers or by an uploaded file.

How to use it: choose the board that fits the moment, then tap a card to hear the phrase or show it on screen. Speech depends on the browser and installed voices.
